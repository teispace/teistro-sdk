`request_json` is `{"system", "length", "breadth", "owner"?, "measure"?}`: `MAYAMATA`, `MANASARA`, `SAMARANGANA` or `CHINTAMANI`, the two sides in whole units (1 to 1 000 000), the owner's nakshatra key, and for `MANASARA` only the measure.
