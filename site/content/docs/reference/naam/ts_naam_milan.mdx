Matches two names star to star (naam milan) and answers with the `naam` blob: each name's first syllable in the śatapada cakra, the varga koota, and the Ashta Koota and the ten considerations of the two name stars (`03-design/matching.md`, C291 to C296).

`request_json` is `{"bride", "groom", "rules"}`: the two names, in Devanagari or in the Latin scheme `rules.name.latin` declares, and the `NaamRules` with every field optional. A name the cakra does not read, a Latin name while `latin` refuses, a `CASUAL` name read more than one way, or an Abhijit syllable while `abhijit` refuses is `INVALID_ARG`, named under `naam.bride` or `naam.groom`.

Reached from `matching.naam` on the surface; [the surface](/docs/surface) says how each binding spells a path.

## Parameters

| name | type | role | what it carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| `context` | `const ts_context *` | the handle | — |
| `request_json` | `const char *` | a string read | — |
| `out_blob` | `ts_blob *` | receives a blob | — |

## What it hands back

`blob`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## The result blob

The call fills a blob following the `naam` schema, which the binding's generated decoder reads. The schema is in `idl/api.json`, and every binding decodes it from the blob's own bytes rather than copying them.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `request_json` NUL-terminated; `out_blob` valid for a write.

Declared in `crates/ffi/src/naam.rs`.
