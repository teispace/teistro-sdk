A guna total graded into bands and said in words (`03-design/matching.md`, "Grading a total into bands", SR-39), answered as canonical JSON `{total, dushkuta, band, bands, row, locale, words, text}`: `band` the band's key (`UNFIT`, `FIT`, `MIDDLING`, `GOOD`, `EXCELLENT`), `bands` the table's name, `row` the row that gave it, `words` the band in words and `text` the total with it.

`request_json` is `{"total"}`, with `"dushkuta"` (whether the signs stand 2/12, 6/8 or 9/5; false when left out), `"shipped"` (a shipped table: `MUHURTA_MARTANDA`, the default, `NEPALI_ALMANAC` or `ARYAVARTA`) or `"bands"` (a consumer's own table, `{name, source, rows}`, each row `{band, from, clears?, bhakoot?}`), and `"locale"` (the context's when left out). A key it does not read, a total outside 0 to 36, an unknown table, both tables or a table that leaves a total ungraded is `INVALID_ARG`, named under `band`; a locale not loaded is `UNSUPPORTED` on `band.locale`.

## What it hands back

## Safety
