Whether a rite may go on during a period of impurity (`03-design/ashauch.md` §12), answered as canonical JSON.

The answer is `{rite, verdict, beginning, row, clause, table}`: `verdict` `GOES_ON`, `IN_PART` or `BARRED`, `beginning` what opens this rite when the texts name it.

`request_json` is `{"table", "rite"}`: `table` the *Dharmasindhu*'s when left out, every row data; `rite` `{rite, begun, noOtherWay, calamity, kind}`. A key it does not read is `INVALID_ARG` named under `rite`; a rite the table names no row for is `UNSUPPORTED`.
