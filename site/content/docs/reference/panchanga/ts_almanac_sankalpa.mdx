The time a sankalpa recites at an instant and a place (`03-design/sankalpa.md`), answered with `{value, provenance}` as canonical JSON, every catalogue member a full key.

`value` is `{at, day, year, ayana, ayanaReckoning, ritu, rituReckoning, month, paksha, tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, sun, moon, jupiter}`: `day` the civil day whose sunrise the instant follows, so a rite before dawn is the day before's; `year`, `ayana`, `ritu` and `month` as that day's sunrise has them, the lunar year with its samvatsara, Vikrama and Shaka numbers and the month under both conventions; the limbs and the Sun's, Moon's and Jupiter's sidereal signs as they stand at the instant.

`request_json` is `{"at", "latitudeDeg", "longitudeDeg", "altitudeM", "utcOffsetSeconds", "ayana"}`: `at` a UTC Julian day, `altitudeM` 0 and `ayana` `SIDEREAL` (turning at Makara and Karka sankranti) when left out, or `TROPICAL` (at the solstices). A key it does not read or an instant, place or offset out of range is `INVALID_ARG`, named under `sankalpa`; a context without an ephemeris is `CAPABILITY`, and an instant in no day's window (a polar day the policy leaves undefined) `OUT_OF_RANGE`.

## What it hands back

`json`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `request_json` NUL-terminated; `out_json` valid for a write.
