The Sun's and the Moon's sidereal longitudes at instants as the panchanga's limbs read them (SR-137, `03-design/panchanga-reach.md` §3), answered with `{value, provenance}` as canonical JSON.

`value` is a list, one `{at, sunDeg, moonDeg, elongationDeg, yogaDeg}` for each instant asked and in its order: the two sidereal longitudes, the Moon less the Sun (the tithi's and the karana's angle) and the Moon plus the Sun (the yoga's), each in degrees from 0 to 360, read in the sky every almanac day's limbs are found in. Every knob of the profile is honoured as the limbs honour it: the ayanamsha and its basis, a custom ayanamsha, the panchanga's centre, true or apparent positions and a provider that defines its own zodiac; so at a limb's end the day's search found, the angle sits on the limb's grid to the search's tolerance.

`request_json` is `{"instants", "latitudeDeg", "longitudeDeg", "altitudeM"}`: one to 4096 UTC Julian days and the place, whose Moon a topocentric panchanga reads; `altitudeM` is 0 when left out. No instants, too many, an instant that is not finite or a place out of range is `INVALID_ARG`, named under `limbAngles`; a context without an ephemeris is `CAPABILITY`.
