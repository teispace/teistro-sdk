A device's camera pointed at the sky (`03-design/sky-pose.md`), answered with `{value, provenance}` as canonical JSON.

`value` is `{sky, pose, verticalFovDeg, screen}`: `sky` is `ts_almanac_sky_view`'s value for the same request, unchanged; `pose` the pose asked, its azimuth in `[0, 360)`; and `screen` is `{grahas, stars, ecliptic, horizon}`, one placement `{inView, x, y, offAxisDeg}` for each point of `sky`'s list of the same name, in its order. `x` and `y` are the point in the rectilinear (gnomonic) picture, normalised to `[0, 1]` with the origin at the top left, outside it beyond the frame and null behind the camera; a graha, a star and an ecliptic point are placed at their refracted altitude, a horizon point at nought. The rising lagna point is `sky.ecliptic`'s `ASCENDANT`.

`request_json` is `ts_almanac_sky_view`'s request with `"pose"` beside it: `{"azimuthDeg", "altitudeDeg", "rollDeg", "horizontalFovDeg", "aspectRatio"}`, the line of sight's azimuth from true north through east (−360 to 360) and altitude (−90 to 90), the roll about it (−180 to 180, positive as the phone turns clockwise seen from its screen), the field across the picture (above 0 and below 180) and the picture's width over its height (0.1 to 10). A missing or out-of-range pose field is `INVALID_ARG` named under `skyPose.pose`, the sky view's own under `skyPose`; the context's refusals are `ts_almanac_sky_view`'s.

## What it hands back

`json`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `request_json` NUL-terminated; `out_json` valid for a write.
