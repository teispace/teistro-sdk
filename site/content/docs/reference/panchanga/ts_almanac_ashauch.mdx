The period of impurity after a birth or a death at a place (`03-design/ashauch.md`), answered with `{value, provenance}` as canonical JSON.

`value` is `{key, kind, relation, sex, start, dayOne, dayOneBy, end, lastDay, length, days, clause, overlap, ritesFrom, table}`: `start` the instant counted from (the event, the cremation, or the hearing), `dayOne` the civil day counted as the first and `dayOneBy` why (daylight, or the night rule that gave it), `end` the instant it ends (a sunrise, a nightfall, or `start` for a bath), `lastDay` the last day it touches, `clause` the table's row or the rule (`REMAINDER`, `LATE`, `ROW_HEARD_LATE`, `BIRTH_PERIOD_RUNS` and the rest) with its text, `overlap` what a period already running did, and `ritesFrom` when the mother of a birth may take part in rites again.

`request_json` is `{"latitudeDeg", "longitudeDeg", "altitudeM", "utcOffsetSeconds", "table", "tradition", "request"}`: `table` the *Dharmasindhu*'s when left out, every row data, or `tradition` a shipped table by name (`DHARMASINDHU`, `MITAKSHARA`), never both; `request` `{key, event, relation, heardAt, abroad, at, fullDays, during}`, its instants UTC Julian days and `fullDays` ten when left out. A key it does not read, or a table or request that cannot be counted, is `INVALID_ARG` named under `ashauch`, as is a fact of the deceased a band needs and the request leaves out; a relation the table names no row for is `UNSUPPORTED`; a context without an ephemeris is `CAPABILITY`, and a polar day `OUT_OF_RANGE`.
