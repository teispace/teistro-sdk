Compares this context's reckoning of a range with another's at one place (`03-design/setup-disagreements.md`) and answers with `{days, festivals}` as canonical JSON, each the `{value, provenance}` of one comparison asked, sealed by this context's provenance, and null when not asked.

`days` is `{otherProfile, otherSettingsHash, compared, days}`: only the days on which some limb's member at sunrise differs, each `{date, tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana}` with a limb null where both name one member, and otherwise `{official, other}`, each side `{member, span, sunrise, change}`: the member it names at its sunrise, that member's span under its sky, its sunrise, and `change`, `{at, minutesFromSunrise}`, the instant the two members meet under that sky, null when the two members are not neighbours. `festivals` is `{otherProfile, otherSettingsHash, compared, observances, ekadashis}`: each occurrence whose day or month differs, or which only one side gives, as `{rule, month, adhika, official, other}` (an Ekadashi with its `tithi` too), each side the observance or fast that side's festival answer gives, or null.

`request_json` is `{"calendar", "first", "last", "latitudeDeg", "longitudeDeg", "altitudeM", "utcOffsetSeconds", "days", "festivals"}`: the days as `{"year", "month", "day"}`, `days` true when left out, `festivals` a festival request, and everything but `first`, the place and the offset optional. A key it does not read, a place or offset out of range, a festival request its reader refuses or a request comparing nothing is `INVALID_ARG`, named under `disagreements`; a null `other` is `INVALID_ARG` naming `other`. A range of more than a year and a day is `OUT_OF_RANGE`, and either context without an ephemeris `CAPABILITY`. A failure of either context's reckoning is recorded on this one.

## What it hands back

`json`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` and `other` must be live handles; `request_json` NUL-terminated; `out_json` valid for a write.
