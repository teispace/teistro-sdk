The blackouts the Sun and the Moon make over a range of civil days by the context's profile (SR-138, `03-design/panchanga-reach.md` §2), answered with `{value, provenance}` as canonical JSON.

`value` is a list of `{kind, at}` in order of their start: `kind` a `blackout_kind` key (`CHATURMAS`, `ADHIKA_MASA`, `SAMSARPA`, `KSHAYA_MASA`, `KHARMAS`, `PITRU_PAKSHA`, `SANKRANTI` or `HOLASHTAKA`), and `at` `{from, to}`, the UTC Julian days it holds over, clipped to the range from the first day's local midnight to the midnight after the last. The rule is the muhurta search's own season, read in the profile's chart zodiac at the range's middle, geocentric, and is in every build.

`request_json` is `{"first", "last", "calendar", "utcOffsetSeconds", "zone", "kinds"}`: `first` and `last` (the first when left out) as `{year, month, day}` in `calendar` (Gregorian when left out), the offset or the zone the midnights are read on (one of them), and the kinds wanted, every one when left out. A kind the Sun and the Moon do not make (asta, an eclipse's star or vedha), both or neither clock field, or a range the wrong way round is `INVALID_ARG`, named under `blackouts`; a context without an ephemeris is `CAPABILITY`.
