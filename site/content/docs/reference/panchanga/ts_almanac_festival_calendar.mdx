A range's observances and Ekadashi fasts at one place as an iCalendar feed (RFC 5545, `03-design/icalendar.md`): one whole-day event each, ordered by day and then rule, its `SUMMARY` the festival's name in the context's locale, its `DESCRIPTION` the rule's source and the answer's provenance, and its `UID` stable for the rule, the day and the place.

`request_json` is `{"calendar", "first", "last", "latitudeDeg", "longitudeDeg", "altitudeM", "utcOffsetSeconds", "festivals", "stampUnixSeconds", "name", "product", "refreshMinutes"}`: the days as `{"year", "month", "day"}`, `festivals` a festival request, `stampUnixSeconds` every event's `DTSTAMP`, and `calendar`, `last`, `altitudeM`, `name`, `product` and `refreshMinutes` optional (`refreshMinutes` a day when left out, none when zero). A key it does not read or one it needs left out, a place or offset out of range, or a festival request its reader refuses is `INVALID_ARG`, named under `festivalCalendar`. A range of more than a year and a day, or a day outside the years 1 to 9999, is `OUT_OF_RANGE`, and a context without an ephemeris `CAPABILITY`. `out_text` is the feed, CRLF lines.

## What it hands back

`text`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `request_json` NUL-terminated; `out_text` valid for a write.
