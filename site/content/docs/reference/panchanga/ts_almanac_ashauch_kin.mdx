The ashauch relation and sapinda degree a path in a family gives (`03-design/ashauch.md` §10), answered as canonical JSON.

The answer is `{relation, keeps, degree, withinGirlsReach, clause, table}`: `relation` what `ts_almanac_ashauch` asks with, absent when no period is kept; `keeps` `PERIOD`, `BATH` or `NOTHING`; `degree`, for one line, `{observer, person, reach}` counted from the kūṭastha, who is 1.

`request_json` is `{"table", "kin"}`: `table` the *Dharmasindhu*'s when left out, every row data; `kin` `{path, observer, personMarried, livingForebears}`, `path` the steps `FATHER`, `MOTHER`, `SON`, `DAUGHTER`, `HUSBAND`, `WIFE` from the observer to the person. A path that steps back, or a fact a row turns on and the request leaves out, is `INVALID_ARG` named under `kin`.
