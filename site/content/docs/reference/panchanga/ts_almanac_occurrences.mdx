Every day a festival rule or a kind of day falls on, year by year over a span of years (`03-design/rule-years.md`), as canonical JSON `{value, provenance}`: `value` is `{keys, years}`, the keys looked for and each year of the span in order as `{year, first, last, days, festivals}`, its first and last days, each day the target falls on with the keys there (none, one or several), and the festival reckoning's entries for the keys in the year, every catalogue member a full key. The span's days are founded once.

`request_json` is `{"fromYear", "years", "latitudeDeg", "longitudeDeg", "altitudeM", "utcOffsetSeconds" or "zone", "calendar", "kind", "rule", "group", "festivals"}`: `years` 1 to 50, counted in `calendar` (Gregorian when left out), which the days are written in too; one of `kind` (`EKADASHI`, `PURNIMA`, `AMAVASYA` or `SANKRANTI`), `rule` or `group`, the last two keys of `festivals`, a festival request. A key it does not read, none or two of `kind`, `rule` and `group`, a rule or group the festivals lack, a place or offset out of range or a festival request its reader refuses is `INVALID_ARG`, named under `occurrences` (a missing rule as `rule`, a missing group as `group`); `years` out of range `OUT_OF_RANGE`, naming `occurrences.years`; a context without an ephemeris `CAPABILITY`.

## What it hands back

`json`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `request_json` NUL-terminated; `out_json` valid for a write.
