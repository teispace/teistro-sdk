Anniversaries kept by the tithi over a span of years, for many people in one call (`03-design/anniversary-span.md`), as canonical JSON `{value, provenance}`: `value` is a festival answer whose `anniversaries` hold each person's days in the order their tithis begin, each `{key, count, kind, origin, monthBy, dayRule, observance}` as a festival request's (`festivals_json`), `dayRule` being `{tithi, at, clause, source}`: the tithi `ORDINARY`, `KSHAYA` or `VRIDDHI`, the window read, the clause that chose the day and where it is stated. Occurrences no day could judge are in `unjudged`.

`request_json` is `{"fromYear", "years", "latitudeDeg", "longitudeDeg", "altitudeM", "utcOffsetSeconds", "calendar", "anniversaries"}`: `fromYear` and `years` (1 to 100) counted in `calendar`, Gregorian when left out, the span running from month 1, day 1 of the first year to the day before month 1 of the year after the last; `altitudeM` optional; `anniversaries` at most 1000, each as a festival request writes one. A key it does not read, a place or offset out of range, an anniversary its check refuses or a key given twice is `INVALID_ARG`, named under `anniversaries`; a `years` out of range `OUT_OF_RANGE`; too many anniversaries `LIMIT`; a context without an ephemeris `CAPABILITY`.
