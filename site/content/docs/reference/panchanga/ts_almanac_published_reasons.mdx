Says why each published day of a festival answer differs from the rule's (`03-design/published-overrides.md` §4) and answers with `{value, provenance}` as canonical JSON, sealed by this context's provenance, every catalogue member a full key.

`value` is `{reasons}`: one per occurrence a `SET` moved, each `{rule, set, day, computed, reason, tried}`, `day` the published day and `computed` the rule's here. `tried` lists each change in the order tried, `{change, day, reproduces}`, `day` the rule's day under the change (absent when it gives none within seven days), and `reason` is the first change that reproduces the published day, or `{"by": "PUBLISHER"}`, the publisher's own choice, when none does. A change is `{"by": "PLACE", "name"}`, `{"by": "SKY", "profile", "settingsHash"}` (`other`'s) or `{"by": "RULE", "pack"}` (each shipped pack whose rule of the key differs).

`request_json` is `{"calendar", "first", "last", "latitudeDeg", "longitudeDeg", "altitudeM", "utcOffsetSeconds", "festivals", "places"}`: the days as `{"year", "month", "day"}`, `festivals` a festival request with its `published` sets, and `places` the places the publisher may have reckoned at, each `{"name", "latitudeDeg", "longitudeDeg", "altitudeM", "utcOffsetSeconds"}`. `other` is the publisher's sky, another context; `context` itself, or null, tries none. A key it does not read, a place or offset out of range or a festival request its reader refuses is `INVALID_ARG`, named under `publishedReasons`.

## What it hands back

`json`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `other` null or a live handle, which may be `context`; `request_json` NUL-terminated; `out_json` valid for a write.
