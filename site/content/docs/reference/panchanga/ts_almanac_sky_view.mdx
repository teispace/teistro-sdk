The sky at a place and an instant (`03-design/sky-view.md`), answered with `{value, provenance}` as canonical JSON.

`value` is `{at, siderealTimeDeg, obliquityDeg, atmosphere, eclipticStepDeg, horizonStepDeg, grahas, stars, ecliptic, horizon}`: each of `grahas` `{graha, raDeg, decDeg, altitudeDeg, refractedAltitudeDeg, azimuthDeg}`, topocentric, apparent and of date, the azimuth from north through east and Rahu and Ketu the node the settings name; each of `stars` the same with `star`, `nakshatra` (the nakshatra it is the yogatara of, or null) and `magnitude`; `ecliptic` `{kind, longitudeDeg, altitudeDeg, refractedAltitudeDeg, azimuthDeg}` at every step of tropical longitude and at the `ASCENDANT` and the `DESCENDANT`, in the order of longitude; and `horizon` `{azimuthDeg, raDeg, decDeg, longitudeDeg, latitudeDeg}` at every step of azimuth from north.

`request_json` is `{"latitudeDeg", "longitudeDeg", "altitudeM", "at", "grahas", "stars", "eclipticStepDeg", "horizonStepDeg", "pressureHpa", "temperatureC"}`: `at` a UTC Julian day, `grahas` and `stars` lists of keys (the nine and the twenty-seven yogataras when left out), each step 0.1 to 90 degrees (5 when left out), and the air 1010 hPa and 10 °C when left out, a pressure of 0 for no refraction. A key it does not read, a place out of range, a step or an atmosphere out of range is `INVALID_ARG`, named under `skyView`; a context without an ephemeris is `CAPABILITY`, and one over a classical astronomy `UNSUPPORTED` (C1185).

| name | type | role | what it carries |

| `out_json` | `ts_string *` | receives a string | — |

## What it hands back

`json`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `request_json` NUL-terminated; `out_json` valid for a write.
