A day for many people at once (`03-design/people-digest.md`), answered with `{value, provenance}` as canonical JSON, sealed by the day's reckoning.

`value` is `{day, sunrise, nextSunrise, nakshatra, moonSign, dasha, people}`: the day's star and the Moon's sign at sunrise, and each person `{name, birthStar, moonSign, lagna, dasha, dashaOpens, transits, hits, tara, chandraHouse, chandrashtama, sadeSati}` in the request's order: the dasha running at sunrise a period a level, the periods opening before the next sunrise, every graha's gochar from their Moon, the day's transit hits on their chart, the day's star counted from their birth star, and the Moon's house from their Moon.

`request_json` is `{"calendar", "day", "latitudeDeg", "longitudeDeg", "altitudeM", "utcOffsetSeconds" or "zone", "people", "dasha", "depth"}`: `day` as `{"year", "month", "day"}` in `calendar` (Gregorian when left out) at the place given; `people` 1 to 50, each `{"name", "instant", "latitudeDeg", "longitudeDeg", "altitudeM", "utcOffsetSeconds"}`, `instant` the birth's UTC Julian day; `dasha` `VIMSHOTTARI` and `depth` 3 when left out. A key it does not read, a place, offset or instant out of range, a name given twice or a depth past 1 to 6 is `INVALID_ARG`, more than 50 people `LIMIT`, each named under `people`; a context without an ephemeris is `CAPABILITY`, as is a build without the chart or the muhurta family.

## What it hands back

`json`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `request_json` NUL-terminated; `out_json` valid for a write.
