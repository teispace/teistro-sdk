When an observance falls in a year or a window under each named tradition (`03-design/observance-finder.md`), the occurrences paired across them, answered with `{value, provenance}` as canonical JSON, sealed by the first tradition's reckoning.

`value` is `{traditions, occurrences, parted, unjudged}`: each tradition `{name, rule, reckoning, profile, settingsHash, provider}`; each occurrence `{month, adhika, kept, agree, differs, reason}`, `kept` a tradition's `{tradition, day, observance}` or `{tradition, day, fast}` in the request's order (no `day` where it keeps none), `differs` among `ABSENT`, `DAY` and `MONTH`, and `reason` `RULE`, `SKY`, `RULE_AND_SKY` or `PUBLISHED` where the days or months part.

`request_json` is `{"calendar", "year", "first", "last", "latitudeDeg", "longitudeDeg", "altitudeM", "utcOffsetSeconds" or "zone", "traditions"}`: a `year` of `calendar` (Gregorian when left out) or the days `first` to `last`; `traditions` 1 to 8, each `{"name", "festivals", "rule", "reckoning"}`, `festivals` a festival request, `rule` a key of it and `reckoning` `THIS` (the default) or `OTHER`, which reckons it under `other`. `other` is null, or `context` itself, when no tradition is reckoned `OTHER`. A key it does not read, a place or offset out of range, a rule `festivals` does not carry, a name given twice or an `OTHER` tradition with no other context is `INVALID_ARG`; more than 8 traditions `LIMIT`; a dated rule `UNSUPPORTED`; each named under `observance`.

## What it hands back

`json`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `other` null or a live handle, which may be `context`; `request_json` NUL-terminated; `out_json` valid for a write.
