A receipt checked (`03-design/receipts-and-datasets.md` §1.3): its record answered again on this context under the receipt's own settings, locale and request, and the value's digest compared, answered as canonical JSON: `{verdict, record, moved, broken, issued, now, reason}`.

`verdict` is `HELD` (the digest is the receipt's), `DIFFERS` (another), `NOT_RERUN` (not answered again; `reason` is `{cause, detail}`, the cause `RECORD`, `PROVIDER`, `SETTINGS`, `LOCALE`, `PACKS`, `OUT_OF_RANGE` or `REFUSED`) or `BROKEN` (a digest the receipt carries is not its own fields'; `broken` names each: `requestDigest`, `settings.hash`, `digest`). `moved` lists each field that moved between the receipt and the check, `{field, issued, now}`: `output` first when the value did, then `sdkVersion`, `calculationVersion`, `catalogueVersion`, `provider` and `tzdb`. `issued` and `now` are the two sides' `{sdkVersion, calculationVersion, catalogueVersion, provider, tzdb}`. An OKF *Attested Computation*'s attester.

`request_json` is the receipt `ts_verify_issue` answered, whole. A key it does not have is `INVALID_ARG`, named under `receipt`; a format other than `teistro.receipt/1` is `SCHEMA_VERSION`, named `receipt.format`.

## What it hands back

`json`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `request_json` NUL-terminated; `out_json` valid for a write.
