A number or an amount of money in words and in figures, by CLDR's rule-based number formats (`03-design/amounts-in-words.md`), answered as canonical JSON: `{text, figure, rules, locale, amount}`, `amount` for an amount the major and minor counts, each in words with its unit word, and whether a decimal was rounded.

`request_json` is `{"number"}` (an integer, a negative spelt with the language's minus) or `{"amount": {"currency", "value", "minorDigits", "rounding", "only"}}`, never both, with `"locale"` (the context's when left out), `"grouping"` (`INDIAN`, `INTERNATIONAL`), `"digits"` (`LATN`, `DEVA`) and `"style"` (`PLAIN`, `VERBOSE`). An amount's value is `{"minor": units}` or `{"decimal": "1234.56"}`, never a float. A key it does not read, both or neither of `number` and `amount`, or a malformed decimal or code is `INVALID_ARG`, named under `inWords`; Sanskrit (not yet reviewed), a grouping or style the language's rules lack, or a currency the locale names no units for is `UNSUPPORTED`.

## What it hands back

`json`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `request_json` NUL-terminated; `out_json` valid for a write.
