A date viewed in a named era: its own calendar's reading, with the year that era gives it in `era` and `era_year`. An era the calendar attaches itself gives the calendar's own reading when that reading is in it; `era.KIRAT_YELE` views a Bikram Sambat date in the Kirat Yele Sambat (`calendars/bikram-sambat.md` §Kirat Yele Sambat), the same year, month and day with the Yele year counted from the epoch the context's `calendars.kirat_yele` names (`KIRATI`, the default, or `YALAMBAR`), turning on Magh 1. An era the calendar cannot give is `UNSUPPORTED` naming `era`, with a hint listing those it can.

## Parameters

| name | type | role | what it carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| `context` | `const ts_context *` | the handle | — |
| `date` | `const ts_calendar_date *` | a struct read | — |
| `era` | `uint16_t` | a value | One of `ts_era`. |
| `out_date` | `ts_calendar_date *` | a struct written | — |

## What it hands back

`date`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `date` valid for a read; `out_date` valid for a read of its `struct_size` and a write.

Declared in `crates/ffi/src/calendar.rs`.
