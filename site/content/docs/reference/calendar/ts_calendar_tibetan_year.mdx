A Tibetan year's names (`calendars/tibetan.md`), as canonical JSON: `{year, rabjung, yearInRabjung, samvatsara, element, gender, animal}`, the rabjung cycle (the first began in 1027) and the year's place in it, 1 to 60, with the Indian cycle's name for that place, and the element, gender and animal it is called by, every member a full key: 2026 is the 40th of the 17th, `samvatsara.PARABHAVA`, Fire–male–Horse. `year` is the one a `TIBETAN_PHUGPA` date carries. A year outside 1027 to 9998 is `OUT_OF_RANGE`, naming `year`.
