The years, months and days from `from` to `to`, both in one calendar, counted as an age is (`03-design/calendar-bikram-sambat.md` §11): the most whole months whose anniversary of `from` is not past `to`, then the days from it, so `ts_calendar_add` of the span to `from` under the same `overflow` gives `to`. An anniversary on a day its month lacks is resolved by `overflow`: one born on the 32nd of Jestha completes a year on the 31st of a 31-day Jestha under `CONSTRAIN`, on the 1st of Ashadh under `CARRY`, and `REJECT` refuses a count from such a day (C806). Before `from`, each part is negative or zero. Dates in two calendars are `INVALID_ARG`, naming `calendar`.

## Parameters

| name | type | role | what it carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| `context` | `const ts_context *` | the handle | — |
| `from` | `const ts_calendar_date *` | a struct read | — |
| `to` | `const ts_calendar_date *` | a struct read | — |
| `overflow` | `uint8_t` | a value | One of `ts_overflow`. |
| `out_span` | `ts_date_span *` | a struct written | — |

## What it hands back

`span`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `from` and `to` valid for a read; `out_span` valid for a read of its `struct_size` and a write.

Declared in `crates/ffi/src/calendar.rs`.
