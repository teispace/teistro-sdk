The date `years`, `months` and `days` after `date`, in its own calendar (`03-design/calendar-bikram-sambat.md` §11): the years and months first, a day number the month lacks resolved by `overflow`, then the days, each count signed. A Bikram Sambat month runs 29 to 32 days, so the 32nd of Jestha plus a month is the 31st of a 31-day Ashadh under `CONSTRAIN`, the 1st of Shrawan under `CARRY`, and `INVALID_ARG` with `NONEXISTENT_DATE` naming `day` under `REJECT`. The ISO week date and the Tibetan calendar, which number no twelve months, are `UNSUPPORTED`; a date past the calendar's span `OUT_OF_RANGE`.

## Parameters

| name | type | role | what it carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| `context` | `const ts_context *` | the handle | — |
| `date` | `const ts_calendar_date *` | a struct read | — |
| `years` | `int32_t` | a value | — |
| `months` | `int32_t` | a value | For example `1`. |
| `days` | `int32_t` | a value | — |
| `overflow` | `uint8_t` | a value | One of `ts_overflow`. |
| `out_date` | `ts_calendar_date *` | a struct written | — |

## What it hands back

`date`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `date` valid for a read; `out_date` valid for a read of its `struct_size` and a write.
