Names checked against a birth's pada, each on its own: where each begins, the pada's own syllable with its initials, whether the two share a star or a pada, whether the name took the pada's substitute, and its clauses (C334, C770, C774). Facts, not a score.

`request_json` is `{"names", "birth", "rules"}`: the names, the birth as `{"nakshatra", "pada"}`, and the `NamingRules` with every field optional. The answer is a list with one entry a name, each `{"check", "refused"}` with one of the two null; a name refused alone is named `naming.names[i]`. A pada outside 1 to 4 refuses the call, named `naming.birth.pada`.

## What it hands back

`json`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `request_json` NUL-terminated; `out_json` valid for a write.
