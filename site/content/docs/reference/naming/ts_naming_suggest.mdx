The names a book suggests for a birth: those whose first syllable is the birth pada's, or reaches it by a paired letter or the pada's substitute, in the cakra's order and then the book's, each with its clauses under the rules; and every name the book holds that the cakra refused. A filter keeps some and never ranks.

`request_json` is `{"bookId" | "book", "birth", "filter", "rules"}`: a book this context loaded, by the id its load answered, or a book carried for this one call; the birth; the `SuggestFilter` (`sex`, `syllables`, `clauses`); and the `NamingRules`. The answer is `Suggestions`. Neither or both of `bookId` and `book`, a book not loaded in this context, a pada outside 1 to 4 or a filter clause the sutra does not report is `INVALID_ARG`, named under `naming`.
