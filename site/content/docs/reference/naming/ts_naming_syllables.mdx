The syllables a pada, a star or a sign names a child by, in the śatapada cakra's order, each with every initial that reaches it: its own letter, a paired letter (C294) and the patro's substitute for a nasal no name begins with (C770). A sign's nine padas, and Makara's four more of Abhijit's (C771), whose `pada` is null.

`request_json` is `{"of", "rules"}`: `of` is `{"of": "PADA", "nakshatra", "pada"}`, `{"of": "NAKSHATRA", "nakshatra"}` or `{"of": "RASHI", "rashi"}`, and `rules` the `NamingRules` with every field optional. The answer is a list of `PadaSyllable`. A pada outside 1 to 4 is `INVALID_ARG`, named `naming.of.pada`; an unknown key is refused by its path under `naming`.

## What it hands back

`json`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `request_json` NUL-terminated; `out_json` valid for a write.
