**The moment of a mundane chart** (`03-design/mundane-charts.md`): an ingress, the lunation before one, an eclipse, a great conjunction or an event on record, and the place it is cast for, as `{value, provenance}` canonical JSON sealed by the chart cast there, with the request as its input.

`value` is `{kind, instant, latitudeDeg, longitudeDeg, altitudeM, utcOffsetSeconds, ingress, solarEclipse, lunarEclipse, name, source}`: `kind` a `mundane_kind` key, `instant` the moment as a UTC Julian day; `ingress` `{kind, instant}` the ingress a lunation precedes; `solarEclipse` or `lunarEclipse` an eclipse's kind; `name` and `source` an event's, as the record gave them. A field a kind does not carry is absent.

`request_json` is `{"kind", "year", "from", "ingress", "event", "latitudeDeg", "longitudeDeg", "altitudeM", "utcOffsetSeconds" or "zone"}`: `kind` a `mundane_kind` key; `year` (Gregorian) or `from` (a UTC Julian day) where the search starts, the first ingress, eclipse or conjunction at or after it; `ingress` the ingress a lunation precedes, `MESHA_SANKRANTI` by default (C1151); `event` `{"name", "instant", "source"}` for an `EVENT`, which reads neither `year` nor `from`. The sankrantis are read in the context's own zodiac, the four tropical ingresses in the tropical one. A key the record does not read, a missing or extra `year`, `from`, `ingress` or `event`, or a place out of range is `INVALID_ARG`, named under `mundane`; an eclipse over a classical astronomy is `UNSUPPORTED` naming `kind`; a context without an ephemeris is `CAPABILITY`.

## Parameters

| name | type | role | what it carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| `context` | `const ts_context *` | the handle | — |
| `request_json` | `const char *` | a string read | — |
| `out_json` | `ts_string *` | receives a string | — |

## What it hands back

`json`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `request_json` NUL-terminated; `out_json` valid for a write.
