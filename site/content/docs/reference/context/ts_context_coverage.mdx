**What this context answers over** (SR-136, `03-design/coverage-edges.md` §5), as canonical JSON: `{ephemeris, bikramSambat, holidays}`. `ephemeris` is `{fromJd, toJd, first, last}`: the first and last instants the configured ephemeris answers under the context's profile, Julian days on UT1, and the first and last Gregorian dates the span holds at least half of, on UT, as `{calendar, year, month, day}`; left out with no ephemeris. `bikramSambat` is `{firstYear, lastYear, officialFirstYear, officialLastYear}`, the shipped table's years and those the government published; `holidays` each shipped holiday set as `{set, years}`.
