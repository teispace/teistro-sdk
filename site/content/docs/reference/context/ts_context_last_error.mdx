The outcome of the last call on the context: its status, the provider's code, and the message, field, hint and key as strings lent until the next call other than this one. After a successful call the record is `OK` with a null message.

```c
ts_status ts_context_last_error(const ts_context * context, ts_error * out_error);
```

No operation of a context calls this by name. A binding calls it itself, opening the library, freeing what the library handed back or reading an error, or offers it outside a context, as the library's version and the fixed-day arithmetic are.

## Parameters

| name | type | role | what it carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| `context` | `const ts_context *` | the handle | — |
| `out_error` | `ts_error *` | a struct written | — |

## What it hands back

`error`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `out_error` valid for a read of its `struct_size` and a write.

Declared in `crates/ffi/src/context.rs`.
