Resolves a civil date-time in a zone to a UTC instant under the context's daylight-saving and unknown-time policies, with the metadata a stored chart keeps. An unknown zone is `UNSUPPORTED` with the nearest names as the hint; a civil time inside a gap under the `error` policy is `INVALID_ARG` with the `DST_GAP` detail.

## Parameters

| name | type | role | what it carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| `context` | `const ts_context *` | the handle | — |
| `civil` | `const ts_civil_date_time *` | a struct read | — |
| `zone` | `const ts_zone_spec *` | a struct read | — |
| `out_resolution` | `ts_zone_resolution *` | a struct written | — |

## What it hands back

`resolution`. The call returns a status; a status other than `TS_STATUS_OK` means nothing was written.

## Safety

`context` must be a live handle; `civil` and `zone` valid for reads; `out_resolution` valid for a read of its `struct_size` and a write.

Declared in `crates/ffi/src/time.rs`.
