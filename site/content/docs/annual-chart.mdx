---
title: The annual chart
description: The Tajika year — the solar return's instant, the chart cast where you name, and the year's office-bearers, sahams and dashas.
---

A birth chart is cast for a birth. An **annual** chart is cast for the
moment the Sun comes back to the longitude it held then: once a year,
never quite on the birthday, and the one instant every Tajika judgement
of that year is made from.

## The instant, then the chart

Whether the year's chart is cast for the birthplace or for where the
native lives now is a question the schools answer differently, so the SDK
answers the instant and casts the chart only where you name a place,
`'birth'` or a residence, in the same `varsha` request:

```js title="bindings/node/example/annual_chart.mjs"
const chart = ctx.chart.found({
  instant: when.instantJdUtc,
  place,
  utcOffsetSeconds: when.offsetSeconds,
  varsha: { reading: 'SIDEREAL', through: 40 },
});
const years = chart.praveshas;
```

```text output="annual_chart"
returns computed: 40
the thirtieth year opens at jd 2458953.185830
between returns: 365.2482 to 365.2644 days
```

Fewer years than you asked for is an answer, not a refusal: an ephemeris
that ends before your hundredth year says so by giving you the years it
has.

## The year's judgement

Cast at the birthplace, a year carries its Muntha, its five
office-bearers and the year lord chosen among them, the matters asked
about, the sahams and the year's own dashas. Each is asked for by name:

```js title="bindings/node/example/annual_chart.mjs"
const cast = ctx.chart.found({
  instant: when.instantJdUtc,
  place,
  utcOffsetSeconds: when.offsetSeconds,
  varsha: { through: 30, place: 'birth' },
}).praveshas[29];
```

```text output="annual_chart"
muntha in VIRGO; office-bearers MERCURY JUPITER MARS VENUS JUPITER, by night
year lord VENUS at 16:29:45, chosen STRONGEST
```

A saham's strength is the source's clauses, reported and never scored
into one number:

```text output="annual_chart"
KARYA_SIDDHI 293.12°  CAPRICORN, lord SATURN, house 3
  strong: LORD_OWN_SIGN, WITH_BENEFIC, LORD_CONJOINS, LORD_ASPECTS_SAHAM, LORD_ASPECTS_LAGNA; weak: WITH_ENEMY, WITH_MALEFIC
```

## Which longitude is a choice with a name

`SIDEREAL` is the tradition's return. `TROPICAL` is the Western solar
return and is most of a circle of lagna away by the fortieth year. `MEAN`
is the older arithmetic and needs no ephemeris at all. None of them is a
fallback for another:

```js title="bindings/node/example/annual_chart.mjs"
for (const reading of ['SIDEREAL', 'TROPICAL', 'MEAN']) {
```

```text output="annual_chart"
SIDEREAL  thirtieth year, 0.00 hours from the sidereal one
TROPICAL  thirtieth year, -10.30 hours from the sidereal one
MEAN      thirtieth year, -0.13 hours from the sidereal one
refused  varsha.through: a return is asked for by the years it completes, 1 to 200, not 0
```
