# Bhava chalit: which method, and how much it matters

Status: `measured`, 2026-09-07. Generated by `cargo xtask chalit` and held by
`check-chalit`; every number below is what this build computes.

The roadmap asks for a falsification pass over the bhava chalit methods before
the chart layer is written. This is it.

A chalit says which *house* a graha is in, as against which sign it occupies.
Four methods are named for it. The question here is not which is correct — no
measurement decides that — but whether the choice matters. If the four agreed to
within a rounding error, the chart layer could pick one and move on.

**They do not agree.** Over 55 charts and 495 graha placements, two methods
built on the same cusps disagree about half the time, and the two closest still
disagree about one placement in five.

## The methods

| method | its sandhi | its madhya | read this way by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sripati** | the `SRIPATI` cusps | the Porphyry cusps | the classical Indian bhava: the quadrants trisected, each cusp the middle of its house, the sandhi halfway between two middles |
| **Vehlow** | the `VEHLOW` cusps | the ascendant and every 30° from it | equal houses centred on the ascendant, so the lagna sits in the middle of the first house rather than at its edge |
| **Porphyry** | the `PORPHYRY` cusps | midway between consecutive cusps | the same trisected quadrants read the Western way, each cusp the start of its house |
| **KP (Placidus)** | the `PLACIDUS` cusps | midway between consecutive cusps | Krishnamurti Paddhati, which takes the Placidus cusps as house starts |
| **whole sign** | the `WHOLE_SIGN` cusps | the middle of each sign | not a chalit at all: the sign the lagna falls in is the whole first house. It is here because it is what a chalit is measured against |

The *sandhi* is the boundary a graha crosses to change house; the *madhya* is
the middle, where a graha is strongest. Two methods can share their cusps and
still be different chalits, because one reads a cusp as a boundary and the other
as a middle: Sripati and Porphyry are exactly that pair, and they are half a
house apart everywhere.

## What the recording engine actually does

The corpus records a `bhava_chalit` for every chart, and every one of them calls
itself `equal-house` (55). That label is wrong, and the geometry says so:

| method | charts whose every recorded placement it reproduces |
|---|---:|
| Sripati | **20** of 55 |
| Vehlow | **55** of 55 |
| Porphyry | **0** of 55 |
| KP (Placidus) | **1** of 55 |
| whole sign | **18** of 55 |

The engine computes **Vehlow** and calls it equal house. They are not the same
thing: equal houses *start* at the ascendant, Vehlow *centres* on it, and the two
are fifteen degrees apart. This is the third entry of the deliberate-difference
registry made exact — `nepali-default` says `VEHLOW` because that is what the
charts it reproduces contain, whatever their label
(`05-testing/01-golden-vectors.md`).

## How far apart the methods are

Every pair, over all 495 placements, and over the 477 of them from the 53 charts
where no system had to be substituted:

| | Vehlow | Porphyry | KP (Placidus) | whole sign |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Sripati** | 21.8% / 21.4% | 50.5% / 50.9% | 51.5% / 50.9% | 29.9% / 28.5% |
| **Vehlow** |  | 51.3% / 50.7% | 50.3% / 50.9% | 21.6% / 21.2% |
| **Porphyry** |  |  | 10.1% / 9.2% | 47.7% / 48.2% |
| **KP (Placidus)** |  |  |  | 47.9% / 49.7% |

Read the table as "how often these two would put a graha in different houses".
Each cell is "over every chart / over the charts where nothing was
substituted". Inside the polar circle a quadrant system has no cusps to give and
whole sign stands in for it, which pulls a quadrant method towards whole sign and
away from an equal method that did not fall back; the second number is the one
without that.

Sripati against Porphyry is the sharpest result here, because those two are the
*same cusps*: the trisected quadrants, read once as house middles and once as
house starts. Their cusps agree to the last decimal and their houses disagree
50.5% of the time. Nothing about the geometry is in dispute between them, only
what a cusp means — and that alone moves half the chart.

Sripati against Vehlow — the two a Jyotisha application would actually choose
between — is 21.8%: better than one placement in five.

## Latitude

Sripati is built on quadrants, which distort as the latitude rises; Vehlow is not.

| latitude | Sripati against Vehlow |
|---|---:|
| under 30° of latitude | 10.8% of 288 |
| 30° and beyond | 37.2% of 207 |

## What this decides

1. **The chalit method is a setting, not a constant.** `houses.chalit_system`
   already exists and already feeds the settings hash
   (`03-design/settings-and-profiles.md`); this measurement is why it must. A
   result that names a house without naming the method is not reproducible.
2. **A house is not a number on its own.** Every placement the chart layer reports
   carries the method that produced it, as every position carries its frame.
3. **The layer reports madhya as well as sandhi.** `astro::houses::Houses` gives
   the cusps and nothing else, which is enough to place a graha and not enough to
   say how near the middle of its house it sits — the thing bhava bala is built
   on. The chart layer's house service returns both.
4. **Whole sign is not a fallback for a chalit.** It disagrees with every method
   here by a quarter to a half of all placements. An application that shows both
   shows two different charts, and should say so.

## How this was measured

For each of the 55 charts of the conformance corpus: the SDK's own
`astro::houses::houses_at` computed each method's cusps from the chart's instant
and place, the cusps were taken back to the sidereal frame with the ayanamsha the
fixture records, and each recorded graha longitude was placed between them. The
SDK's cusps reproduce the recording engine's within an arcsecond over this same
set (`crates/astro/tests/baseline_houses.rs`), so the disagreements here are
between the methods and not between the engines.

The polar policy is `FALLBACK_WHOLE_SIGN`, and 2 of the 55 charts needed it. A
substituted method is not that method, so every pairwise number is given twice:
once over everything, and once over the charts where nothing was substituted.
Quote the second.
