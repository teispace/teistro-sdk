## 2. The year's count

The year is the civil year an estimate of its middle falls in, less 880:
the day's sunrise moved by the months between it and the year's middle.
Held here to the lunar years the almanac itself finds
(`almanac().years`), whose Vikrama number less 937, or less 936 from
Kachhala on, is the Nepal Sambat year: on every day from 1 October to 5 December
of every 20th civil year from 1810 to 2390, over the committee's
sky, which holds each year's last days of Kaula and first of Kachhala,
where the estimate stands furthest from the day.
