## Past the obliquity

A planet off the ecliptic can stand further from the equator than the
Sun ever does. Leo gives this no reading, and the page decides none; it
counts how often each body does. The Sun's own latitude is the
topocentric frame's parallax, a few arcseconds, so a birth at a solstice
can carry it past by that much.
