## Antiscia and parallels

Two degrees in antiscion have the same declination, so two planets in
antiscion would stand in parallel if neither had a latitude. The table
counts how often a pair holds both, on the same side for the antiscion
and on opposite sides for the contrantiscion, the parallel within Leo's
1° (p. 47). The moieties and Leo's orbs are many degrees wide and hold
pairs no 1° parallel could, so the last rows read the antiscia at the
parallel's own 1°. Without latitude, a 1° antiscion is always a
parallel too, since a degree of longitude moves a declination by at most
0.4°; so the pairs that fall short do so by their planets' latitudes,
which an antiscion does not read.
