# `teistro-matching`

Matching two births through the Moon of each, as the Teistro SDK reads
it. There is no ephemeris here: a native is the Moon's nakshatra, pada,
sign and navamsha, and every table is transcribed from a cited source.

- **Ashta Koota**: the eight kootas of *Muhurta Chintamani* VI.21–34,
  worth 36 points, with the points *Daivajna-manohara* gives them as the
  *Piyushadhara* commentary cites it. Each koota reports what it read,
  and Bhakoot and Nadi report their dosha and each exception's clause.

The design is `docs/03-design/matching.md`.
