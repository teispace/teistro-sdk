## Not a second copy of the geometry

`astro::houses` computes twenty-two systems with their polar outcomes,
`chart::bhava` makes bhavas of any system's cusps, and both are already
measured against the corpus. This crate is the layer that answers what a
caller asks and nothing else answered — and the falsification pass found
its subject by looking for the parts of `houses.*` **nothing had read**.

## What the pass found

**A boolean cannot say what happened.** The engine records
`is_degenerate` — one bit for "the chosen system had no solution here" —
and the SDK's `Outcome` has three cases. Nothing had ever compared them,
and they disagree **in both directions**:

| | latitude | |
|---|---|---|
| the engine flags, the SDK computes | 64.15°, 64.84° | Reykjavik and Fairbanks, Placidus |
| the engine leaves clear, the SDK cannot | 69.65° | Tromsø, where whole sign stood in |

The polar circle is 66.56°. The engine flags *below* it, where Placidus
is defined, and not *above* it, where it is not — so the two are not the
same quantity read to different precision. They disagree about which
charts are the difficult ones. Registry entry 26.

**The shift, counted the other way.** The engine lists the bodies the
chalit moves out of their whole-sign house; `chart`'s test checks every
body it lists, and nothing checked that the SDK lists no *others*. Both
directions now hold, on the same 135 bodies over 75 fixtures.

**A knob that was worse than unread.** `houses.module_overrides` says
which system a named module uses — and the root *populates* it, so all
five shipped profiles carry `kp → PLACIDUS` and nothing had ever asked.
Under the default profile the rest of the chart is whole-sign, so a KP
reading was quietly the wrong chart rather than an error.

**Two fields that simply hold.** `cusp_sign_index` is the sign each
cusp's own longitude falls in, exactly, on all 852; and the recorded
midheaven is the SDK's within 0.0022°.

## What it decides

- **A degenerate chart is reported, not refused.** Otherwise the polar
  policy would mean nothing. `Houses::with_outcome` carries what the
  house computation said, and `is_defined()` is the caller's check.
- **A house takes the sign its *middle* falls in.** Under an unequal
  division a house can begin in one sign and be centred in another, and
  the lord follows the middle.
- **The three quadrants partition the twelve** and the trikona, dusthana
  and upachaya cut across them — so one enum for the partition and
  predicates for the rest, with the partition asserted rather than
  assumed.

## Tests

26: 12 beside the rules they test, 3 over the corpus, 8 end to end over
a founded chart, and 3 doc examples.

- `tests/baseline.rs`: the shift counted both ways over all 75 fixtures
  that carry a chalit and positions, and the three degeneracy
  disagreements asserted **as** disagreements — the test fails if a
  fourth appears or one of the three goes away.
- `tests/chart.rs`: the bhavas, the quadrants partitioning them, both
  readings placing every body, a degenerate chart reported rather than
  refused, and the shipped `kp` override actually read.
