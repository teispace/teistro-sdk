# `teistro-hellenistic`

The Hellenistic and traditional Western doctrine of the Teistro SDK.
It has no ephemeris, no chart and no instant: a planet's essential
dignities at a longitude are a reading of tables, and every table here is
transcribed from a cited source and measured against it in
`docs/03-design/terms-measured.md`.

- **Terms**: the Egyptian and the two printings of Ptolemy's own as
  tables, the Chaldean as the rule *Tetrabiblos* I.XXIII states, and a
  consumer's own table, refused by sign when it is malformed.
- **Essential dignities**: house, exaltation, triplicity, term and face,
  detriment and fall, as flags; a school's scores, Lilly's among them, as a
  separate value.

The design is `docs/03-design/essential-dignities.md`.
