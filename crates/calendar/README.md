# teistro-calendar

The calendars of the Teistro SDK: the fixed day every calendar converts
through; proleptic Gregorian and Julian, the mixed calendar with its
transition, the ISO week date; Bikram Sambat over its table (the official
span as the authority publishes it, every other year of 1700 to 2500 BS
computed by the SDK's own engine, every date saying which answered); and
the solar-calendar engine itself: a `SolarModel` (the Surya Siddhanta
today), the sankranti finder over the shared solver, the month-start
rules of the traditions as rows, and the measurement that chose the
punya-kala rule against the official table.

```rust
use teistro_calendar::{BikramSambat, CalendarSystem, Gregorian};

let day = Gregorian.to_fixed_ymd(2024, 4, 13).expect("a real date");
let bs = BikramSambat::shipped().date_of(day).expect("in the table");
assert_eq!((bs.year, bs.month, bs.day), (2081, 1, 1));
```

Design: `docs/03-design/calendar-gregorian-julian.md`,
`calendar-bikram-sambat.md`; the source memo
`docs/calendars/bikram-sambat.md`. The table is generated by
`cargo xtask gen calendars` from `data/bikram-sambat.json` and the
engine, and held by `cargo xtask check-calendars`.
