# `teistro-western`

The modern Western doctrine of the Teistro SDK. It has no ephemeris and
no chart: the arithmetic that matches a span of the sky's time to a span
of a life's is a reading of the measure alone, and every measure here is
held to Alan Leo's *The Progressed Horoscope* (1906).

- **Progressions**: a day for a year, a day for a month and a month for a
  year, or any rate, under the tropical or the Julian year or Leo's own
  rule by sidereal time at noon, each answered both ways.
- **Arc measures**: Ptolemy's degree for a year, Naibod's mean motion of
  the Sun, or any rate.

The design is `docs/03-design/western-progressions.md`.
