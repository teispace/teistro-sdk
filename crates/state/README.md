# `teistro-state`

Status: `built`, 2026-09-07. The design is
[`docs/03-design/state-and-avasthas.md`](../../docs/03-design/state-and-avasthas.md);
the measurement that tested it before a line of this crate existed is
[`docs/03-design/state-tables-measured.md`](../../docs/03-design/state-tables-measured.md),
generated by `cargo xtask state` and held by `check-state`.

What a graha *is*, as opposed to where it is: dignity, the three
friendships, combustion, the ages, the planetary war and the avasthas.

| module | what it settles |
|---|---|
| [`dignity`](src/dignity.rs) | the ladder, the natural, temporary and compound friendships, the dispositor |
| [`burn`](src/burn.rs) | combustion: the two shipped orb tables and what they make of a distance from the Sun |
| [`avastha`](src/avastha.rs) | the five ages, wakefulness, the deeptadi, the lajjitadi, the war and who wins it |
| [`boundary`](src/boundary.rs) | how near a body stands to a sign, nakshatra or pada edge |
| [`chart`](src/chart.rs) | the assembly: every graha of a founded chart, with what it is |

No port, no ephemeris, no instant. Everything here is arithmetic over a
founded chart, which is why the whole crate is testable against the
corpus without a provider.

## What the corpus decided

**837 readings of nine grahas over 93 fixtures**, and the recorded
dignity, friendships, combustion, age, wakefulness, war and lajjitadi
are the answer. Four things the measurement settled that a reading of
the texts alone would not have:

1. **Moolatrikona is checked before exaltation.** Three grahas have a
   moolatrikona span inside their exaltation sign, and taking
   exaltation first is wrong on every reading in one.
2. **A body in its own sign is its own friend.** The catalogue cannot
   say it — a graha appears in none of its own three lists — so the
   rule lives here.
3. **The ages alternate.** Six-degree fifths run forward in an odd sign
   and backward in an even one; reading them forward everywhere is
   wrong on 359 of 837.
4. **The shadow grahas take a reduced ladder** that ends at neutral,
   never at a friendship dignity.

Two differences from the recording engine are asserted **as**
differences rather than skipped, and both are in the deliberate
difference registry: the deeply debilitated body the engine records as
dreaming rather than asleep, and the lagna, which the engine gives
placeholder friendships to and this crate does not treat as a graha at
all.

## Two combustion tables

`state.combustion_orbs` names one. Both carry the Surya Siddhanta's
degrees of time as the outer orb (IX.6 to 8; the Moon's, X.1) — the
same six numbers `astro`'s heliacal visibility reads, held to them by a
test so the two copies cannot drift — and they part on what lies
inside:

| key | orb | deeper orb |
|---|---|---|
| `SURYA_SIDDHANTA` | the degrees of time | none: the text gives none |
| `BPHS` | the same six numbers | an inner orb the corpus brackets |

The default profile names the first (ADR-0024, "the texts as read"), so
**under it no body is ever deeply combust**. Over the corpus that is one
change in the conservative direction: the same sixty-six bodies burn,
and the thirty-six the recording engine calls deeply combust come back
merely combust. `Combustion::orbs` reports the orbs a reading was judged
against, and whether there was a deeper one at all.

## What it will not answer

Six avasthas are not decidable from a founded chart, and the crate
returns nothing where it cannot decide rather than a plausible guess.
They are the ones whose definitions read "or aspected by": the deeptadi
below its top three, and three of the six lajjitadi, which
`Lajjitadi::undecided` names on every reading. They wait on `aspect`.

The same reasoning shapes `boundary`: it reports the **distance** to the
nearest sign, nakshatra and pada edge and ships no threshold. A constant
compiled into the library could not answer "is this classification safe"
for two providers of different accuracy, so the tolerance is the
caller's to state.

## Tests

43 of them: 29 beside the rules they test, 7 over the corpus,
6 end to end over a founded chart, and the crate doc's own example.

- `tests/baseline.rs`: every recorded dignity and friendship, every
  combustion reading, every age, wakefulness and lajjitadi, every war
  and its victor, the boundary distances against the bracket the corpus
  gives, both registered differences asserted as differences, and the
  second orb table over the whole corpus.
- `tests/chart.rs`: the assembled state is the state of the position the
  foundation gave it, a war is symmetric across the whole chart, the
  deeptadi is absent exactly where the dignity does not decide it, an
  unknown orb table is refused by name, and two runs of one chart are
  the same value.
